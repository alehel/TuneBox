# Attribution

TuneBox's only third-party assets are two fonts: Lato for the interface, and Noto Sans Symbols 2
for the symbols Lato lacks. Every icon, image and sound is its own.

| Asset | Origin | Licence |
|---|---|---|

| Visualizer looks | Drawn in code: `crates/visualizer/src/looks/` | Project licence |
| Demo album covers | Generated procedurally by `xtask/src/covers.rs` when the demo is built; not committed | Project licence |
| Demo audio, album and artist names | Generated by `xtask/src/demo.rs`; all names are invented | Project licence |
| Screenshots in `docs/images/` | Taken of TuneBox running the demo (`cargo xtask screenshots`) | Project licence |

The OFL lets the fonts be bundled and redistributed with the app; the font files themselves may
not be sold on their own, and a modified font may not be called "Lato".

Dependencies and their licences are checked by `cargo deny check` against `deny.toml`. The one
copyleft dependency is symphonia (MPL-2.0), used unmodified inside craft-audio to decode MP3,
FLAC, ALAC and Vorbis.

Any asset added later must be original or openly licensed and listed here in the same change
(see [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) §5).
